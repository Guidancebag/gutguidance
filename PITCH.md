# GutGuidance - Y Combinator Application

## One-Liner
AI that finds your hidden food-symptom triggers in days, not the 6 years it currently takes.

---

## The Problem

**70 million Americans** suffer from digestive diseases (IBS, IBD, SIBO, GERD, food intolerances). The current diagnostic journey is broken:

- **Average time to diagnosis: 6+ years** across 3-4 specialists
- **Paper food diaries** are tedious, imprecise, and doctors spend 30 seconds reviewing weeks of data
- **Generic elimination diets** (like low-FODMAP) eliminate 70+ foods when most patients react to only 3-5 specific triggers
- **83% of patients** report having food triggers they cannot identify
- **$136 billion** spent annually on GI healthcare in the US alone

The result: millions of people living in constant discomfort, afraid to eat, unable to get answers.

---

## The Solution

**GutGuidance** is an AI-powered gut health platform that turns vague digestive complaints into personalized, actionable protocols.

### How It Works:
1. **Log meals & symptoms** - Takes under 60 seconds. Our food database auto-categorizes by FODMAP rating and inflammatory score.
2. **AI correlation engine** - Statistical analysis of food-symptom timing, combinations, and severity finds YOUR unique triggers within 7-14 days.
3. **Personalized elimination diet** - Not a generic list. A custom protocol that removes only YOUR trigger foods while keeping your diet diverse and enjoyable.
4. **Continuous optimization** - As you log more data, recommendations get sharper. Track your Gut Health Score improving in real-time.

### Key Differentiator:
We don't guess. We use YOUR data to find YOUR patterns. Average time to first actionable insight: **7 days** vs. **6 years** with traditional approaches.
